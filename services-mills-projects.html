What Mills Projects Does

The Mills Projects manages project cargo, breakbulk, heavy-lift and out-of-gauge shipments where a single missed requirement can disrupt the entire project schedule. From cargo engineering, permits and customs clearance to port handling, lifting, heavy-haul transport, route surveys, site access and final positioning, The Mills coordinates each movement as one controlled project plan, protecting project timelines, managing interface risks and preventing costly execution gaps from origin to site.

Where clients usually get stuck

  • One missed requirement stops the scheduleA permit, route restriction or lifting limit discovered late can delay an entire project.
  • Cargo that does not fit the routeOut-of-gauge pieces meet bridge, road and site-access limits that were never surveyed.
  • Many parties, no single planShippers, port, trucking and site teams each hold a piece, and no one holds the whole.

How It Works

How The Mills Manages Project Cargo

01

We understand the cargo and the project: The Mills reviews the cargo list, technical drawings, dimensions, weights, shipment mode, origin and required delivery schedule.

02

We identify what must be cleared before movement: Tariff classification, permits, duty and tax treatment, port requirements and delivery constraints are mapped early.

03

We build the movement plan: The required lifting method, transport equipment, route, site access, handling sequence and contingencies are coordinated with qualified providers.

04

We manage the cargo through arrival and delivery: The Mills coordinates customs clearance, discharge, cargo release, lifting, heavy-haul transport and final project-site delivery.

05

We close out the shipment properly: Delivery records, cost documentation, exceptions and reference points for succeeding shipments are organized as part of the project file.
