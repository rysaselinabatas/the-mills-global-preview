What Mills Advisory Does

The Mills Advisory supports decisions that do not begin or end with a single customs entry. It helps management understand the regulatory path, import structure, customs exposure and government workstreams affecting a product, transaction, expansion or recurring trade program.

Where clients usually get stuck

  • Unclear starting pointManagement knows a transaction needs regulatory clearance but not which agencies apply, in what order, or how customs fits in.
  • Decisions made before the rules are mappedSupplier contracts, importer structure and pricing get fixed before anyone confirms what the Philippines actually requires.
  • Exposure found too lateAudit findings, classification disputes and permit gaps often surface after the cargo has already moved.
