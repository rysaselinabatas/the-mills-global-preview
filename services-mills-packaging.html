What Mills Packaging Does

The Mills Packaging provides export and project-cargo crating for machinery, heavy equipment, fragile and high-value goods, using cargo-specific wooden cases, skids, pallets, blocking, bracing, securing, moisture protection and ISPM 15-compliant treated wood where required. Each packing method is matched to the cargo dimensions, weight, lifting points, transport mode, route conditions, storage period and final handling requirements.

Why Businesses Choose The Mills

You get a coordinated plan, not disconnected agency instructions.

  • Packaging is designed around the actual cargo and journey.
  • Customs inspection, carrier handling and insurance evidence are considered.
  • Industrial and project cargo receive stronger protection than ordinary commercial packing.
  • Condition and packing records support accountability if damage occurs.

Packaging specifications depend on accurate cargo information, inspection, route, mode and qualified-provider assessment. No packaging method eliminates all transport risk, and insurance coverage must be reviewed separately.
