What Mills Packaging Does

The Mills Packaging provides export and project-cargo crating for machinery, heavy equipment, fragile and high-value goods, using cargo-specific wooden cases, skids, pallets, blocking, bracing, securing, moisture protection and ISPM 15-compliant treated wood where required. Each packing method is matched to the cargo dimensions, weight, lifting points, transport mode, route conditions, storage period and final handling requirements.

Where clients usually get stuck

  • Packing chosen after the quoteCrating is treated as an afterthought, then falls short on weight, lifting points or route conditions.
  • Damage in transitFragile and high-value cargo moves with inadequate blocking, bracing or moisture protection.
  • Packing rules missedTreated-wood and packing requirements can hold cargo up when they are not planned for.
