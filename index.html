A customs broker prepares and files import and export entries with the Bureau of Customs, classifies goods, computes duties and taxes, secures the required permits, and coordinates examination and release so the cargo can move.

The Mills serves data centers, renewable energy, construction, manufacturing, automotive, healthcare, food and beverage, agriculture, chemicals, electronics and telecom, marine, aviation, retail, cold chain, mining and freight forwarders.
