What Mills Entry Does

The Mills Entry is for businesses that need to operate, import, distribute or expand in the Philippines but do not yet have the full corporate and regulatory structure required to begin. The service connects business establishment to customs and product readiness so the first shipment is planned as part of market entry, not treated as an afterthought.

Where clients usually get stuck

  • Buying before the structure is readyCompanies order stock before deciding who the importer of record is or which registrations apply.
  • Registrations in the wrong orderIncorporation, business permits and importer registration depend on each other and are easy to sequence badly.
  • A first shipment that becomes a testWithout a plan, the first regulated shipment becomes how the company finds out what was missing.
