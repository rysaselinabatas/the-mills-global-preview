Philippine Market Entry & Import Readiness

Enter the Philippine market with the right structure, registrations, import readiness and first-shipment plan from the beginning.

Start Philippine Market Entry

What Mills Entry Does

The Mills Entry is for businesses that need to operate, import, distribute or expand in the Philippines but do not yet have the full corporate and regulatory structure required to begin. The service connects business establishment to customs and product readiness so the first shipment is planned as part of market entry, not treated as an afterthought.

Who This Is For

See if this sounds like you.

  • Foreign companies entering or expanding in the Philippines
  • New Philippine importers and exporters
  • Manufacturers appointing a local subsidiary or distributor
  • Investors evaluating PEZA, BOI or ordinary domestic operations
  • Businesses preparing a first regulated-product shipment
  • Companies replacing an informal or high-risk import structure

Core Support

Here's exactly what's included.

01

Market-entry structure review

Map the intended Philippine business, ownership, importer, distribution and operational model before registrations begin.

02

Company incorporation coordination

Support the establishment of the appropriate Philippine entity through the relevant corporate and professional workstreams.

03

Business registration support

Coordinate SEC or DTI registration, BIR registration, local permits and other requirements needed to become operational.

04

Importer and exporter readiness

Prepare the corporate, financial, customs and facility information required for the applicable BOC registration or accreditation.

05

Product and industry permit mapping

Identify the FDA, DA, BIR, DTI, NTC, DENR or other approvals that may apply to the proposed products.

06

PEZA and BOI coordination

Map available registration, customs and incentive-related requirements for qualified projects and enterprises.

07

First-shipment planning

Align importer status, permits, classification, value, documents, route, insurance, customs and delivery before the first booking.

08

Post-registration compliance

Organize renewals, reportorial obligations, changes in business information and continuing agency requirements.
