Mills Time Critical

Priority Clearance & Expedited Cargo Execution

Priority customs and logistics coordination for cargo that cannot afford ordinary processing timelines.

Activate Priority Support

What Mills Time Critical Does

The Mills Time Critical is an escalation service for shipments where delay can stop production, ground equipment, hold a vessel, interrupt medical operations or create disproportionate commercial loss. It prioritizes pre-arrival review, clear ownership, fast decisions and disciplined exception management.

Who This Is For

See if this sounds like you.

  • Airlines and maintenance providers handling AOG parts
  • Shipowners and operators requiring vessel spares
  • Hospitals and healthcare suppliers
  • Data centers and critical-infrastructure operators
  • Project owners facing milestone or outage exposure

Core Support

Here's exactly what's included.

01

Urgency assessment

Confirm the operational consequence, latest useful delivery time, permits and non-negotiable dependencies.

02

Pre-arrival document review

Identify customs, permit and consignee issues before cargo reaches the Philippines where possible.

03

Priority clearance coordination

Escalate filing, assessment, examination, payment and release follow-through within lawful channels.

04

AOG shipments

Coordinate qualified aircraft-on-ground parts and supporting aviation or customs requirements.

05

Vessel spares

Manage urgent customs, port-access and vessel-delivery coordination.

06

Production-critical parts

Prioritize components needed to prevent or address operational stoppage.

07

Medical and infrastructure cargo

Coordinate urgent qualified medical, data-center, power and utility equipment.

08

Expedited delivery

Arrange suitable priority transport, handover and delivery reporting.

09

Escalation reporting

Maintain named owners, current blockers, next action and decision requirements.

How It Works

How The Mills Executes The Engagement

01

Confirm the cargo, consequence of delay, latest acceptable delivery time and decision-makers.

02

Review importer, permits, classification, value and documents immediately.

03

Create the shortest lawful execution path and identify dependencies that cannot be compressed.

04

Run active clearance, carrier, port and delivery escalation with concise milestone reporting.

05

Close the file with delivery proof, exception analysis and prevention actions for repeat cargo.

Why Businesses Choose The Mills

You get a coordinated plan, not disconnected agency instructions.

  • Urgency is translated into a controlled execution plan rather than panic.
  • Decision-makers receive the blocker, owner and next action clearly.
  • Customs, port and delivery workstreams are managed together.
  • The service distinguishes controllable response time from government or carrier processing.

Priority handling is not a guarantee of customs release or delivery within a fixed period. Timelines remain subject to government processing, inspections, permits, carrier and terminal performance, weather, security, force majeure and other circumstances outside reasonable control.
