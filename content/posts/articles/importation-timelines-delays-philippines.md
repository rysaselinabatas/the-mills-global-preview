Knowing the typical timelines involved in Philippine importation helps businesses plan their supply chains and reduce the risk of unexpected delays. While timelines vary depending on the goods, the port of entry, and the completeness of documentation, the general stages follow a predictable sequence.



## Typical Importation Timeline



### Pre-Arrival (2-4 weeks before shipment)

- [Secure BOC accreditation](/articles/boc-accreditation-requirements-philippines) (if not yet registered)
- Obtain [regulatory permits from relevant agencies](/articles/import-permits-philippines) (FDA, DA, DTI, etc.)
- Prepare and finalize all shipping documents



### Shipment in Transit (days to weeks depending on origin)

- Sea freight from Asia: approximately 5-14 days
- Sea freight from the US/Europe: approximately 20-35 days
- Air freight: approximately 1-5 days



### Arrival and Port Processing (1-5 working days, standard)

- Goods declaration filed upon or before arrival
- Lane assignment by the customs selectivity system
- Green Lane: release within 1-2 working days
- Yellow Lane: documentary review adds 1-3 working days
- Red Lane: physical inspection adds 3-7 working days or more



### Payment and Release (1-2 working days after assessment)

- Duties and taxes assessed and payment processed
- ATRIG issued for regulated goods
- Delivery order secured from shipping line
- Port charges settled and gate pass issued



## Common Causes of Delays

- Incomplete or inaccurate goods declaration
- Missing or late permits from regulatory agencies
- Discrepancies between documents (value, quantity, description)
- Physical inspection (Red Lane assignment)
- Late BOC accreditation or NRI application
- Port congestion or vessel delays
- Outstanding storage or arrastre charges



## How to Minimize Delays

- Secure permits and accreditation well before the shipment departs
- Submit complete and accurate documentation
- Work with a [licensed customs broker](/articles/role-of-customs-broker-philippines) who knows the system
- Monitor your shipment status and respond quickly to any requests from customs



Most delays in Philippine customs are avoidable with proper planning. The earlier you prepare your documents and permits, the smoother your clearance will be.
