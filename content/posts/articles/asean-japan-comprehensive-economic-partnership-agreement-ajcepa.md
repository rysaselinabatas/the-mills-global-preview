The ASEAN-Japan Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (AJCEPA) is a free trade agreement between the ten ASEAN Member States and Japan. It was established to strengthen economic relations, expand market access, improve trade facilitation, and promote investment cooperation between ASEAN countries and Japan.



The agreement covers Trade in Goods (TIG), Sanitary and Phytosanitary Measures (SPS), Technical Barriers to Trade (TBT), Trade in Services, Trade in Investment, and Economic Cooperation and Trade Facilitation. AJCEPA aims to gradually establish a stronger free trade area over a ten-year period while supporting ASEAN economic integration. Philippine importers dealing with Japanese goods should also review the bilateral [Philippines-Japan Economic Partnership Agreement (PJEPA)](/articles/philippines-japan-economic-partnership-agreement-pjepa-guide), which may offer additional benefits.



## Basic Principles of AJCEPA

- Participation of all ASEAN Member States and Japan
- Promotion of liberalization, trade facilitation, and cooperation
- Reciprocity, transparency, and mutual benefit
- Consistency with World Trade Organization (WTO) rules
- Recognition of different development levels among ASEAN members
- Flexibility for sensitive sectors in ASEAN countries and Japan
- Support through technical cooperation and capacity building



## Objectives of AJCEPA

- Strengthen economic integration between ASEAN and Japan
- Improve the global competitiveness of ASEAN and Japan
- Liberalize trade in goods and services progressively
- Create a transparent and liberal investment environment
- Promote new areas of economic cooperation
- Reduce development gaps among ASEAN Member States



## Trade Facilitation and Cooperation Areas

AJCEPA also includes cooperation in strategic sectors:

- **Customs and Trade Procedures** - Simplification, harmonization, and computerization of customs procedures to speed up trade flows. This reduces the risk of [demurrage and detention](/articles/demurrage-detention-management) charges caused by processing delays.
- **Business Environment** - Improving the regulatory and investment climate to attract investors.
- **Intellectual Property Rights** - Support for ASEAN countries in improving IP systems and joining international IP agreements.
- **Energy Cooperation** - Collaboration in oil stockpiling, natural gas use, and energy efficiency.
- **Information and Communications Technology (ICT)** - Development of ICT infrastructure, legal systems, and IT human resources.



## Philippine AJCEPA Tariff Highlights

Based on the tariff schedule, a large majority of Philippine tariff lines under AJCEPA are at 0% tariff rates from 2018 onwards, covering around 88.5% of tariff lines. This reflects the Philippines' commitment to reducing trade barriers and increasing access to Japanese and ASEAN markets. ## Why AJCEPA Matters to Philippine Businesses

AJCEPA provides Philippine exporters, importers, and investors with significant opportunities through lower tariffs, easier customs procedures, and stronger economic cooperation with Japan and ASEAN partners. It helps businesses become more competitive while encouraging foreign direct investment and technology transfer. For Philippine companies involved in manufacturing, agriculture, electronics, automotive parts, and services, AJCEPA remains an important framework for regional trade growth.
