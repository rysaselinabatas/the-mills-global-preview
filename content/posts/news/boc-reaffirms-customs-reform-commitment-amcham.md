## BOC Outlines Digital Transformation Roadmap in AmCham Dialogue

The Bureau of Customs (BOC) has reinforced its commitment to optimizing the Philippine trading environment during an executive dialogue with the American Chamber of Commerce. The bureau's strategic roadmap relies heavily on digital transformation to lower operational friction and drive port efficiency.

Key structural upgrades impacting multinational enterprises include the development of the automated Customs Processing System (CPS) and the activation of the Top Importers Office - a dedicated unit designed to provide high-volume corporate accounts with predictable, expedited customs workflows. These initiatives reflect a concerted effort to institutionalize transparency and support cross-border business continuity.
