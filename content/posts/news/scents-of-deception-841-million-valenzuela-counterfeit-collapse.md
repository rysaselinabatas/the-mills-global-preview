## Philippine Authorities Seize ?841M Counterfeit Perfumes in Valenzuela

Philippine authorities successfully seized ?841 million worth of counterfeit perfumes and lotions mimicking luxury international brands during a warehouse raid in Valenzuela. The operation revealed an extensive counterfeit operation distributing fake versions of high-end brands across local markets.

The operators face severe criminal charges for violating the country's Intellectual Property Code and the Customs Modernization and Tariff Act, leading to the total forfeiture of the property. While the facility has been sealed and secured, no immediate arrests were made, leaving agents to track down the true source of the goods and the warehouse owners.
