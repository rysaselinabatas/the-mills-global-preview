At the ASEAN Growth Forum held on June 12, 2026, Bureau of Customs (BOC) Commissioner Ariel F. Nepomuceno outlined plans to fully digitalize the Customs Processing System and begin integrating artificial intelligence into select customs services. For Philippine importers and freight forwarders, this points to a customs environment that is moving, gradually but deliberately, toward automation.



## What BOC Announced

The digitalization plan covers the core Customs Processing System that handles the bulk of import and export transactions in the Philippines. According to Commissioner Nepomuceno, the goal is to improve transparency, streamline processing, enhance trade facilitation, and strengthen BOC's ability to detect and combat smuggling and other illicit trade activity.

AI integration, still described as being rolled out to "selected services" rather than the entire system, is expected to support functions such as risk profiling and document review, areas that currently rely heavily on manual verification during the [customs clearance process](/articles/customs-clearance-process-in-the-philippines-explained).



## The IAM Agenda

Nepomuceno tied the announcement to BOC's broader Integrity, Accountability, and Modernization (IAM) agenda, and emphasized that closer collaboration between government and the private sector will be central to making the shift work. That collaboration matters in practice: system changes of this scale tend to affect how brokers file entries, how documents are submitted, and how quickly shipments move through processing.



## What Importers Should Watch For

- Announcements on which specific customs services will get AI-assisted processing first.
- Updated technical requirements for electronic submissions as systems are upgraded.
- Possible changes to [import entry filing](/articles/import-entry-filing-process-philippines) procedures as automation expands.

Digitalization initiatives like this one tend to roll out in phases rather than all at once. Businesses that keep their documentation clean and their filing practices consistent now will be in a stronger position to adapt once new digital tools become mandatory, rather than scrambling once the deadlines are announced.
