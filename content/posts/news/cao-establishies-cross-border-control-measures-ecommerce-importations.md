Customs Administrative Order (CAO) No. 01-2025, issued by the Bureau of Customs, establishes cross-border control measures for e-commerce importations. This order aims to regulate and monitor the influx of goods purchased online from international sources, ensuring compliance with customs regulationsand facilitating efficient processing of such shipments.
