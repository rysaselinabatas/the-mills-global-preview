## DA Imposes Safeguard Duties on Imported Meat, Coffee, and Onions

The Department of Agriculture has imposed safeguard duties on imported meat, coffee, and onions after prices of these products fell below government-set thresholds. The move aims to protect local farmers and producers from cheaper imports that may affect domestic markets.

Under the new measure, additional duties will apply to selected imported agricultural products. Officials said the policy is intended to help stabilize local industries while ensuring fair competition in the market. The safeguard duties complement the DA's ongoing push for stronger import protection across key agricultural commodities.
