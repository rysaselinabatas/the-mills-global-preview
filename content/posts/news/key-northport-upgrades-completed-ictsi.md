## ICTSI Advances NorthPort Modernization with Key Milestone Tests

International Container Terminal Services, Inc. (ICTSI) completed key testing milestones for the NorthPort upgrade program, with Pier 16 tests confirming compliance with engineering and safety standards. Terminal 1 rehabilitation works remain on track for end-June 2026 completion, while the overall modernization program is scheduled for completion by end-2027.

The NorthPort upgrade is expected to improve cargo handling efficiency, strengthen port infrastructure, and support faster and more reliable trade and supply chain operations in the Philippines.
