## FedEx Breaks Ground on Expanded Clark Gateway for Asia Pacific Operations

Federal Express Corporation (FedEx) has started construction on a major expansion of its Clark gateway facility, a move the company says will reinforce the Philippines' role as a key trans-shipment hub in its Asia Pacific network and improve connectivity for local businesses to global markets.

When finished, the upgraded 78,000+ square meter site will house advanced handling systems to increase capacity for express, e-commerce, and freight shipments and bolster network resilience amid rising regional cross-border trade volumes.
