## ATI Reports Strong Cargo Growth, Projects Continued Rise in 2026

Asian Terminals Inc. (ATI) expects continued cargo growth in 2026 after posting strong double-digit increases in container volumes at its Manila South Harbor and Batangas Container Terminal in 2025. Manila South Harbor handled 1.463 million TEUs last year, up 13% from 2024, while Batangas Container Terminal processed 307,000 TEUs, a 14% increase and its second-highest annual throughput since 2019.

For 2026, ATI projects a 4% rise in Manila South Harbor volumes driven mainly by sustained import demand, while Batangas is expected to achieve another double-digit increase due to larger vessels and more South Luzon locators shifting cargo through the terminal.
