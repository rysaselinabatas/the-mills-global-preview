## PPA Introduces Fuel Adjustment Mechanism for Pilotage Services

The Philippine Ports Authority (PPA) introduced a fuel adjustment scheme for pilotage services to address the impact of fluctuating fuel prices on port operations nationwide. The agency said the new mechanism would allow pilotage fees to adjust in line with fuel cost movements, helping sustain efficient maritime navigation and ensure uninterrupted services in ports across the country.

Under the policy, the PPA aims to assist port pilots and service providers in managing operational expenses while maintaining the safe movement of vessels in major gateways. The measure forms part of the agency's efforts to strengthen the sustainability of port operations and uphold reliable maritime services amid continued volatility in fuel prices.
