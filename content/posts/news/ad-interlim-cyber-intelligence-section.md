CMO No. 1, series of 2025, issued by the Commission on Higher Education (CHED), outlines the updated policies and guidelines for the implementation of flexible learning in higher education institutions (HEIs) across the Philippines.



This memorandum emphasizes the integration of digital technologies and innovative teaching methodologies to enhance educational delivery. It mandates HEIs to develop comprehensive flexible learning plans that ensure accessibility, inclusivity, and quality of education for all students. Additionally, it provides directives on faculty training, infrastructure development, and continuous assessment to adapt to evolving educational needs.
