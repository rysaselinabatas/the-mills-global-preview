## BOC Deploys New Tactical Officers for Enhanced Border Security

The Bureau of Customs (BOC) has deployed a new batch of tactical officers to strengthen border protection and improve enforcement operations across the country's ports and entry points. The initiative aims to enhance the agency's capability to prevent smuggling, illegal trade, and other customs violations while supporting national security efforts.

The newly assigned officers underwent specialized training to prepare them for high-risk operations, inspections, and intelligence-driven missions. Their deployment reflects the government's continued push to modernize customs enforcement and ensure tighter monitoring of goods entering and leaving the Philippines.
