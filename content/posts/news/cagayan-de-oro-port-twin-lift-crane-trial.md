## Port of Cagayan de Oro Tests New Twin-Lift Harbor Crane for Improved Efficiency

The Port of Cagayan de Oro is taking a significant step toward faster and more efficient cargo operations after testing a newly acquired twin-lift harbor crane designed to improve cargo-handling capacity. The trial signals a major boost in port productivity as operators aim to streamline vessel turnaround times and meet increasing logistics demands in Northern Mindanao.

The twin-lift harbor crane is expected to enhance operational efficiency by allowing simultaneous lifting of cargo containers, reducing delays and improving overall port performance at one of Mindanao's key commercial gateways.
