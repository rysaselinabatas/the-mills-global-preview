Customs Memorandum Circular (CMC) 32-2025 addresses the circularization of lost or misplaced accountable forms, specifically Bureau of Customs Form No. 38A ("Official Receipt"). The memorandum, issued on February 5, 2025, ensures that these lost documents are properly recorded and not misused.
