# The Mills Global — Custom Visual Asset Prompts

Each prompt below is **fully self-contained** — paste any single one into your image generator (Midjourney, DALL·E, Ideogram, Firefly, etc.) on its own with no other context needed. Brand/style direction is repeated in every prompt on purpose so you never have to rebuild it.

## How these get used on the site
Each asset is a **layered "sticker" cutout** (transparent background PNG), not a full photo. The idea: place it on top of a photo/color panel in a section so the subject visually pops off the layer beneath it (e.g. a ship's hull physically overlapping the horizon line of a sea-texture panel behind it, like a collage). Export at minimum **2000×2000px, transparent PNG**, subject filling most of the frame with clean edges for easy cutout/masking.

Shared brand direction repeated in every prompt: dark teal (#1E323A) and construction orange (#F15A24) as the only accent colors; editorial/documentary photography style, not cartoon or illustration; matte finish, soft directional lighting, subtle film grain; no text, no logos, no watermarks; realistic proportions, no surreal distortion.
