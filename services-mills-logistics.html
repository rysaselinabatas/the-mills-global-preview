Coordinate Philippine Logistics

What Mills Logistics Does

The Mills Logistics takes over once the cargo is ready to move, coordinating port pickup, drayage, warehousing, staging, inland or inter-island transport and final delivery. The service keeps the cargo, documents, handling requirements and delivery schedule aligned from release to destination.

Who This Is For

See if this sounds like you.

  • Importers requiring delivery after customs release
  • Exporters moving cargo to ports, airports or consolidation points
  • PEZA and economic-zone enterprises
  • Project-cargo and industrial clients
  • Cold-chain and regulated-product businesses
  • Freight forwarders needing Philippine execution support

Core Support

Here's exactly what's included.

01

Port drayage

Coordinate container or loose-cargo movement from the port to a warehouse, ecozone, facility or delivery point.

02

Domestic trucking

Arrange suitable local transport for containerized, loose, refrigerated, high-value or specialized cargo.

03

Dry storage

Coordinate temporary or longer-term storage for non-temperature-sensitive cargo.

04

Cold-chain storage

Arrange qualified temperature-controlled storage and handling based on documented product requirements.

05

Bonded warehousing

Coordinate eligible movements to or from qualified customs-controlled facilities.

06

Cargo staging

Organize cargo for inspection, distribution, packaging, installation or sequenced project delivery.

07

Handling and equipment

Coordinate loading, unloading, forklifts, cranes or other approved handling resources.

08

Final delivery

Manage movement to the consignee, warehouse, plant, store, vessel or project site.

09

Proof and exception reporting

Capture delivery records, damage observations, delays and outstanding action items.

How It Works

How The Mills Executes The Engagement

01

Confirm cargo release conditions, dimensions, weight, packaging, destination and special handling needs.

02

Select the appropriate qualified transport, facility and equipment providers.

03

Align pickup windows, port documents, gate access, permits and delivery instructions.

04

Monitor movement, exceptions, handover and proof of delivery.

05

Reconcile third-party records, waiting time, damage notes and remaining compliance actions.

Why Businesses Choose The Mills

You get a coordinated plan, not disconnected agency instructions.

  • Clients deal with one coordinated shipment plan from customs release to final delivery.
  • Cargo is matched with the right handling, storage and transport requirements before movement begins.
  • Delivery conditions are checked early, helping avoid delays, rehandling and last-minute changes after release.
  • Clients have clearer visibility over timing, responsibilities and costs throughout the movement of the cargo.

Delivery, warehousing, trucking, handling, lifting and related logistics services are arranged as part of the shipment plan, based on cargo requirements, location and operating conditions.
