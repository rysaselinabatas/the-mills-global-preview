What Mills Logistics Does

The Mills Logistics takes over once the cargo is ready to move, coordinating port pickup, drayage, warehousing, staging, inland or inter-island transport and final delivery. The service keeps the cargo, documents, handling requirements and delivery schedule aligned from release to destination.

Where clients usually get stuck

  • A gap between release and deliveryCargo is cleared but nobody owns the trucking, storage and receiving that follow.
  • Handling conditions lost on the wayTemperature, equipment and delivery-window requirements are easy to drop between providers.
  • No proof when something goes wrongWithout delivery records and exception reports, disputes with carriers and consignees drag on.
