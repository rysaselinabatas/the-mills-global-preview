General disclaimer

Information on this website is general and may not reflect the requirements applicable to a particular product, shipment or transaction. Government approvals, customs release, carrier acceptance, insurance coverage and delivery timelines remain subject to applicable law, agency evaluation, inspections, third-party performance and circumstances beyond The Mills Global’s reasonable control. Engagement scope, professional responsibility and applicable partner services are confirmed separately in writing.

Services performed directly vs. through partners

Specialized transport, lifting, warehousing, packaging, surveying and insurance services may be performed directly or coordinated through qualified and appropriately licensed partners, depending on the cargo and service requirements. Where a service is reserved to a licensed professional (legal representation, insurance underwriting, engineering sign-off), it is provided by that qualified third party, not by The Mills directly.

No guarantee of government approval

The Mills coordinates permit assessment, applications and customs filings, but does not issue or guarantee any government approval. Applicability, documentary requirements, inspections, processing and approval remain subject to current law and the responsible Philippine agency. Priority or time-critical handling is likewise not a guarantee of release or delivery within a fixed period.

Legal, tax and other reserved services

The Mills Advisory and related coordination services do not replace legal, tax, engineering, insurance or other regulated professional advice. Reserved services are separately provided by duly qualified professionals, including Maulit Law where applicable, under their own engagement.
