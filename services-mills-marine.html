Mills Marine

Marine, Maritime & Vessel Coordination

Coordinated regulatory and customs support for vessels, marine equipment, port operations and maritime businesses.

Consult Mills Marine

What Mills Marine Does

The Mills Marine manages the importation and regulatory process for vessels and marine equipment, covering customs, permits, port requirements, registration and related operational needs. It is designed for commercial and leisure marine clients that need these requirements handled as one coordinated process.

Who This Is For

See if this sounds like you.

  • Shipowners, operators and ship managers
  • Yacht, leisure-vessel and marina clients
  • Marine contractors and offshore operators
  • Importers of engines, navigation equipment and vessel machinery
  • Barge, tugboat, LCT and workboat owners
  • Suppliers of urgent vessel spares

Core Support

Here's exactly what's included.

01

MARINA coordination

Coordinate applicable registrations, authorities, documentary submissions and vessel-related requirements.

02

PPA and port coordination

Manage relevant port documentation, access, cargo and operational requirements.

03

Vessel importation

Map customs, tax, registration, ownership, class and technical documentation for eligible imported vessels.

04

Yacht and leisure-vessel support

Coordinate qualified importation, documentation, registration and local compliance requirements.

05

Marine equipment customs clearance

Handle permits, classification, valuation and clearance of engines, electronics, parts and marine systems.

06

Inter-island shipping support

Coordinate applicable domestic maritime documents and operational requirements.

07

Vessel-spares delivery

Prioritize clearance and port or vessel delivery of critical repair and operating parts.

08

Marine insurance coordination

Connect clients to appropriate marine and cargo insurance options through licensed partners.

How It Works

How The Mills Executes The Engagement

01

Identify the vessel, equipment, transaction, owner, registry, port and intended Philippine operation.

02

Map MARINA, PPA, customs, tax, class, insurance and technical requirements.

03

Organize documents and responsible parties before arrival or transfer.

04

Coordinate the approved filing, customs, port, handling and delivery workstreams.

05

Close the file with registrations, clearances, proof of delivery and continuing obligations.

Why Businesses Choose The Mills

You get a coordinated plan, not disconnected agency instructions.

  • Vessel and cargo requirements are handled through one coordinated maritime file.
  • Customs clearance is connected to the intended registration and operation.
  • Urgent spares can move through a defined time-critical escalation path.
  • Specialist partners are disclosed where class, survey, engineering or insurance work is required.

Vessel registration, licensing, survey, class, port access and insurance remain subject to the responsible authorities and licensed providers. Eligibility must be confirmed from the actual vessel, ownership, intended operation and supporting documents.
