What to bring to the call

A short call is often enough to tell whether a shipment is routine or needs closer review. Come with whatever you already have (the product, a rough timeline, and any documents on hand) and The Mills will map out what’s actually required from there.

  • The product or cargo type, and its intended use
  • Country of origin and, if known, the supplier
  • Any permits, certificates or documents already in hand
  • Your target ship date or delivery deadline
