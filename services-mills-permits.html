What Mills Permits Does

The Mills Permits maps each product and shipment against the applicable regulatory requirements, identifying the responsible agencies, required approvals, pre-shipment conditions, customs-aligned documentation and continuing obligations after approval.

Where clients usually get stuck

  • Not knowing which agency appliesThe same product can touch several regulators, and its commercial name rarely tells you which.
  • Approvals that arrive too latePermits and registrations take time, and regulated cargo cannot be released without them.
  • Approvals that do not match the shipmentPermit details that differ from invoices, labels or customs entries cause delays.

Core Support

Here's exactly what's included.

01

Permit mapping

Identify the regulator, establishment license, product registration, shipment clearance and supporting documents potentially applicable to the product.

02

FDA coordination

Support License to Operate, Certificate of Product Registration and other applicable health-product authorization workstreams.

03

BIR ATRIG coordination

Prepare and coordinate Authority to Release Imported Goods applications for covered articles and excise-tax situations.

04

Agricultural sanitary and phytosanitary requirements

Coordinate requirements involving BAI, BPI, BFAR, and NMIS, including importer licensing or registration, sanitary and phytosanitary clearances, quarantine requirements and inspection, as applicable. For meat importation specifically, NMIS licenses qualified meat importers, while the SPS Import Clearance is processed through BAI.

05

Product standards and certification

Coordinate DTI-BPS requirements for products subject to mandatory certification, including the Import Commodity Clearance (ICC), Philippine Standard certification, inspection, testing and supporting product documentation.

06

Telecommunications and optical media requirements

Coordinate registrations, import permits and equipment approvals involving NTC and OMB, including NTC type approval, type acceptance or equipment conformity requirements and applicable OMB import or export permits.

07

Environmental, chemical and controlled-goods requirements

Coordinate applicable requirements involving DENR-EMB, PDEA, DDB, and DOST-PNRI for regulated chemicals, controlled precursors, radioactive materials and environmentally regulated goods.

08

Specialized regulatory requirements

Coordinate permits, clearances and registrations involving FPA, PCA, SRA, NTA, MARINA, PPA, DTI-FTEB, LTO, CAAP, DENR-BMB and DENR-FMB, depending on the product and shipment.

09

Renewals and amendments

Track permit validity, renewals, amendments, additional product registrations and continuing regulatory requirements after approval.
