About The Mills Global

A Philippine Trade-Execution Firm Built for Complex Cargo

A Philippine trade-execution firm built for regulated, complex and high-consequence cargo.

Who We Are

Coordinated Philippine trade execution, from readiness to release.

The Mills Global coordinates the legal, regulatory, customs and operational requirements surrounding Philippine trade. The firm supports companies entering the market, importers securing product approvals, project owners moving industrial cargo and businesses that need disciplined execution beyond routine customs clearance.

The Mills is designed around a simple principle: cargo should not move before the business understands what the transaction requires. That means identifying the importer structure, permits, tariff treatment, documents, handling conditions, risk controls and delivery plan before preventable exposure reaches the port.

What Makes The Mills Different

Six things clients notice about how we work.

  • We get involved early: The Mills can step in before the cargo is booked to review the importer setup, permits, product requirements, documents and shipment plan.
  • We make the requirements clear: Clients are told what approval is needed, which agency handles it, what documents are required and what has to happen first.
  • Management gets a usable executive summary: For more involved shipments, The Mills consolidates the key requirements, open issues, risks, responsibilities and next actions into a concise management view.
  • Complex cargo is handled based on what it actually requires: Project cargo, oversized machinery, breakbulk, vessels, dangerous goods, FMCG and urgent shipments are assessed individually rather than treated as standard cargo.
  • The work does not stop at customs release: The Mills also handles the key post-clearance requirements needed to properly close out the shipment and address any customs or documentary issues that may follow.
  • The different parts of the shipment stay connected: Where trucking, crating, insurance, surveying, engineering or another specialist is needed, The Mills coordinates the sequence and keeps responsibility clear.

How The Mills Works

Assess the shipment. Secure the requirements. Clear the cargo. Coordinate release and delivery.

01

Discovery: The Mills collects information about the company, product, supplier, route, shipment and intended Philippine use.

02

Requirement mapping: The Mills identifies likely corporate, permit, customs, insurance and delivery requirements.

03

Readiness review: Missing, inconsistent or high-risk items are raised before execution.

04

Execution plan: Responsibilities, documents, dependencies, target milestones and escalation points are organized.

05

Coordination: The Mills manages the approved customs and cargo-support workstreams directly or through disclosed qualified partners.

06

Close-out: Shipment records, outstanding permits, renewals, claims documents and compliance actions are organized.
