Dangerous Goods & Regulated Cargo Compliance

Dangerous goods managed through proper identification, permits, documentation, packaging and controlled movement.

Assess Dangerous Cargo

What Mills Dangerous Goods Does

The Mills Dangerous Goods is for cargo whose chemical, physical or regulatory characteristics require more than an ordinary shipping instruction. The service connects technical product information to Philippine permits, customs treatment, transport documentation, packaging, labeling, storage and qualified-provider acceptance.

Core Support

Here's exactly what's included.

01

Cargo identification

Review the product, composition, intended use, technical data and Safety Data Sheet to identify likely transport and regulatory concerns.

02

Classification coordination

Coordinate confirmation of the applicable dangerous-goods classification through competent and qualified parties.

03

Permit mapping

Identify possible DENR-EMB, PDEA, PNRI, FDA, BIR, FPA and other regulatory requirements.

04

Documentation

Coordinate declarations, safety information, packing evidence, certificates and carrier documents.

05

Packaging and labeling

Arrange appropriate packaging, markings, labels and shipment preparation through qualified providers.

06

Carrier acceptance

Coordinate required pre-approval and documentation with capable air, sea or inland providers.

07

Customs clearance

Align regulatory documents, product description, classification and customs entry information.

08

Storage and delivery

Coordinate qualified facilities, handling controls and final delivery requirements.

09

Incident documentation

Organize records and escalation if leakage, damage, rejection or another exception occurs.

How It Works

How The Mills Executes The Engagement

01

Obtain the current Safety Data Sheet, technical product data, quantity, packaging and intended use.

02

Confirm the regulatory and transport classification through the appropriate qualified review.

03

Map permits, carrier acceptance, packaging, labels, customs and facility requirements.

04

Approve the shipment only after dependencies and responsible providers are identified.

05

Coordinate arrival, customs, handling, delivery and file close-out under the agreed controls.

Why Businesses Choose The Mills

You get a coordinated plan, not disconnected agency instructions.

  • The service begins with technical identification, not a generic freight quote.
  • Regulatory and transport requirements are aligned before carrier acceptance.
  • Packaging, customs, storage and delivery remain connected in one file.
  • The Mills states clearly where a qualified classifier, carrier, facility or technical provider is required.

Dangerous-goods classification, packaging, transport and handling remain subject to current regulations, accurate shipper information, carrier acceptance, facility capability and verification by qualified service providers. The Mills does not replace the shipper's legal responsibility to disclose the cargo correctly.

Common Questions
